Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Short version: we collect what you type into our forms, use it to do the work you asked for, and don't sell it.

1. What we collect

What you give us. When you fill out a form on this site — the free test request or the contact form — we collect what you type: your name, work email, company, company domain, competitors, and your message.

What our hosting collects automatically. Like nearly every website, our hosting provider records standard technical logs to keep the site running: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps.

We don't collect payment details on this site — payments are handled separately and directly with you.

2. How we use it

We use your information to: deliver the free test and scorecard you requested; reply to your messages; and carry out any service you buy. That's it. We don't sell your information, rent it, or share it with marketers. We don't add you to a newsletter — there isn't one.

3. Who else sees it

Only the service providers needed to operate the site and deliver the work — for example, our hosting provider and, once connected, the form-delivery service. They see only what's necessary to do their job. We'll also share information if the law requires it.

4. Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't use tracking cookies, analytics, or advertising pixels. The site's fonts load from Google's servers (Google Fonts), which may log basic technical data according to Google's own policies.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as needed to serve you — to deliver a test, complete a project, or answer a question — and then delete it on request (see below). Business records we're legally required to keep (like invoices) follow the applicable retention rules.

6. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to: show you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Use the contact form and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

7. Children

This site and our services are for businesses, not children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will post the new version here with a new date. If a change materially affects how we handle your information, we'll note it here.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: use the contact form.
